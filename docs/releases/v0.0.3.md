## NBX-go v0.0.3 — faster GO (beta)

### What changed
- **GO is instant for everyday requests.** "send 5 HBAR to Alice", "swap 100 HBAR to PLEX", "check my transactions", "what did I receive?", "balance" are understood from keywords and contact names without running the model; GO asks only for what's missing.
- **Transaction history in GO and History** now covers HBAR you sent (Hedera EVM transactions) and every NBX-go token, with saved contacts shown by name.
- **Swap fix:** swaps from the platform-fee account no longer fail (Hedera rejects a fee transfer to yourself); errors are now short and readable.
- **Models page:** a dropdown per model type with Download & load / Load now, and a "keep NBX-go open" warning while models download.
- Token logos are bundled — they load instantly and offline.
- Profile account card restyled as an orange NBX-go card; Settings uses a gear icon.
